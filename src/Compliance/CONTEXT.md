# Compliance

Compliance determines whether project parties and workforce declarations satisfy Quebec construction obligations.

## Language

**CCQ Declaration**:
A workforce declaration evaluated against CCQ trade and regional rules.
_Avoid_: Timesheet, report

**Subcontractor**:
An external construction business participating in a project.
_Avoid_: Supplier, worker

**RBQ Licence Result**:
The current validity assessment of a subcontractor's RBQ licence.
_Avoid_: Scrape result
